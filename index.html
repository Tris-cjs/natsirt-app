Install

  • 1. Tap Download above, then open the downloaded file
  • 2. Android asks about installing unknown apps → allow it
  • 3. Tap Install, open Natsirt, and search for any song

What you're installing

  • Streams music from the web — an independent player, not affiliated with any streaming service. It plays whatever you search for, ad-free, from each phone's own connection.
  • Collects nothing. No account, no login, no tracking. Your listening history stays on your phone only.
  • Updates itself. If the app ever stops working, that means the streaming service changed something — the fix arrives automatically through the built-in updater.
  • Runs entirely on your phone. There's no central server; every device is independent.
Natsirt is a hobby project. If something breaks, it usually fixes itself within a few days.
